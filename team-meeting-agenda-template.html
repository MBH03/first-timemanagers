Team Meeting Agenda Template

A recurring team meeting without a written agenda turns into whoever talks first setting the direction. This keeps it structured without turning it into a production.

Agenda itemOwnerTimeNotes
Icebreaker[name]5 min
Metrics review[name]5–10 min
Project update[name]10–15 min
Open flag or ask[name]5–10 minAnyone can add a specific problem that needs the group's input
Shout-outopen to anyone2 min
Rotate who owns the meeting itself
Not just the agenda items, someone new runs the actual meeting each week. It spreads ownership and keeps you from being the bottleneck for something this routine.
Keep the icebreaker and the shout-out
They cost five minutes total and they're usually the only part of the meeting that isn't a status update. Don't cut them first when time is tight.
Write real notes, not just attendance
A blank notes column after the meeting is a sign nothing got decided. If a discussion happened, capture the actual outcome, not just that it occurred.
