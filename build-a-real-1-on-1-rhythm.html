Day 90 · Move 02

Build a real 1:1 rhythm.

Same time, same structure, every week. The rhythm itself builds trust before any single conversation does — cancelling early trains people to expect it'll keep happening.

A 1:1 that gets rescheduled twice in its first month teaches your team it's optional. The rhythm is doing work before a single conversation happens inside it — it's proof you'll show up consistently, which is the thing actually being tested in someone's first few weeks with a new manager.

Step 1 — Fix the day, time, and length first
Consistency matters more than optimizing the format on day one. Pick a slot and protect it like you would a client meeting.
Step 2 — Give them the first few minutes
Whatever's on their mind, not your agenda. Open every 1:1 with your list and you train people to save the real conversation for somewhere else.
Step 3 — Keep a running shared doc, not a fresh page each time
Ongoing notes let both of you track commitments across weeks instead of starting from zero every time.
Step 4 — Treat a cancellation as a real cost
If you have to move it, reschedule within the same week rather than letting it slip to "whenever." The pattern you set in month one is the pattern that sticks.
