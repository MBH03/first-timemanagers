Stage 04 · Year 1

Making It Yours

Moving from surviving the role to actually shaping how your team works.

01
Decide what you'd change if it were fully yours
By year one, you have enough standing to actually make calls, not just execute the ones you inherited. Start being deliberate about which of those inherited habits you keep.
02
Build the thing nobody assigned you
The best first-year managers usually build one process, ritual, or system nobody asked for and everyone ends up using. That's the clearest signal the role is actually yours now.
03
Start developing your own manager
Someone on your team may be close to where you were a year ago. Start treating their growth as part of your job, not a distraction from it.
04
Write down what you'd tell yourself on day one
Not for anyone else — it's how you notice how much has actually changed, and what you'd genuinely do differently if you were starting over today.
