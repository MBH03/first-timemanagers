Your team isn't gelling.

Four moves for a team that's underperforming as a group, even if individuals are fine.

01
Check whether it's a trust problem or a clarity problem
Teams that don't trust each other look identical to teams that don't understand who owns what. They need completely different fixes, so diagnose before you act.
02
Create one shared win early
A small, visible thing the whole team accomplishes together does more for cohesion than a values exercise or an offsite ever will.
03
Address the one person quietly checked out
A single disengaged person can flatten a team's energy more than any process issue. Deal with it directly instead of hoping the team culture fixes it.
04
Model the behavior you want mirrored
Teams gel around what their manager visibly does, not what their manager says they value. If you want candor, be visibly candid first.
