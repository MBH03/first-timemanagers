Welcome to AI.

You were hired to do this job. AI wasn't. That's worth remembering before anything else here, because it's a genuinely powerful tool, but only once it knows its actual place.

Before you ask the machine for an answer, sit with the problem yourself first. Think your own thoughts, even the half-formed, probably-wrong ones. They'll be rough. That's fine, they're yours, and yours is exactly what a good AI conversation needs to have something to push against.

Used that way, it stops being a machine you ask for answers and becomes something closer to a sparring partner: you bring the first draft of your thinking, it pokes at the weak spots, and what comes out the other side is sharper than either of you would've landed on alone.

Before you start

Talk to it, don't just paste a question in and copy out the answer. Keep doing some of your work the old-fashioned way, so your own instincts don't go soft. Have some fun with it. And share what you're learning, everyone's figuring this out at roughly the same speed.

The rest of this hub is organized around that idea: how to think about it day to day, how to actually use it as a thinking partner instead of an answer machine, and how to set it up once for the work you do the same way every week.
