Onboarding Plan (30-60-90) & Offboarding

A structured first-90-days onboarding plan that blends learning with action. New hires alternate between self-directed learning they complete on their own schedule and collaborative sessions with their manager, buddy, and teammates, with clear milestones and explicit expectations from day one. Includes an offboarding checklist for when someone leaves.

Offboarding Checklist

Departing employee: [Full name] · Last day: [MM / DD / YYYY]

  • ☐ Knowledge transfer — document open projects, key contacts, and where files live; record a handoff walkthrough.
  • ☐ Access revocation — schedule removal of systems, email, VPN, and building access for the last day.
  • ☐ Exit interview — hold a candid conversation; capture feedback on role, manager, and culture.
  • ☐ Team communication — share the news with the team (with the employee's input on timing and message) and reassign responsibilities.
  • ☐ Final paperwork — confirm final pay, benefits transition, equipment return, and signed documents.
  • ☐ Farewell — thank them sincerely; wish them well publicly if they're comfortable with it.

Start knowledge transfer the moment a departure is known, not the final week. How someone leaves is how the remaining team judges the culture.
