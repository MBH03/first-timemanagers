Career Advice

Switching industries or functions.

Four moves for making a real jump, not a general essay on reinventing yourself.

01
Translate your track record, don't just list it
"Managed a team of six" means nothing without the outcome attached. Rewrite your experience in the language of the field you're moving into, not the one you're leaving.
02
Lead with transferable judgment, not domain trivia
You won't out-know a domain veteran on day one, and you don't need to. What transfers is how you diagnose problems and make decisions under uncertainty.
03
Get one real conversation with someone who made the jump
Job postings won't tell you what actually matters in the new field. A thirty minute conversation with someone who's already crossed will save you months of guessing.
04
Expect a demotion in scope before a promotion in trajectory
A lateral or even smaller-scope role in the new field is often the fastest real path in, not a step backward.
