Career Advice

Interviewing while you're still employed.

Four moves for running a search discreetly, not a general essay on when to tell your boss.

01
Decide your discretion boundaries up front
Know who at work, if anyone, is going to know you're looking, and stick to that list. Decide it once, deliberately, instead of improvising it conversation by conversation.
02
Protect your calendar without lying about it
Block time vaguely: "personal appointment", rather than inventing specific cover stories that are easy to accidentally contradict later.
03
Don't let a live process rush a decision at work
An interview loop in progress isn't leverage yet. Don't threaten to leave, or start checking out, before you actually have a written offer in hand.
04
Have your references ready before you need them
Give your references a heads-up early so they're not caught off guard by a call, and so you're not scrambling to line them up at the final stage.
