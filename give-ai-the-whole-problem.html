AI Hub · The New Skill

Stop searching.
Start giving AI the whole problem.

We learned how to search for answers. AI changes that.

The advantage is no longer knowing the perfect keywords. It's knowing what information to give AI so it can actually help you think.

Instead of searching for the answer, give AI the situation.
The email thread. The customer complaint. The performance data. The project plan. The messy notes. The competing opinions.
Then ask it to help you make sense of all of it.

That's the new search skill.

The Skill Nobody Taught You

Search rewarded a narrow skill: guess the right keywords, sift through ten results written for someone else's situation, and hope one of them happens to be close enough to yours. It never actually knew your team, your numbers, or what happened in that meeting last Tuesday.

AI can know those things, if you tell it. The skill isn't asking a better question anymore. It's deciding what to hand over before you ask anything at all.

What "The Whole Problem" Actually Means

Not a summary of the situation. The situation itself, in its original, unedited form.

The email thread
Every reply, not your one-line summary of it. The actual disagreement is usually buried in the back-and-forth, not the subject line.
The customer complaint
Their exact words. Your paraphrase already strips out the part that tells you how angry they actually are.
The performance data
The real numbers, including the ones that don't make your case. AI can't catch what you've already filtered out.
The project plan
The actual document, owners and dates included, not your verbal summary of what you think it says.
The messy notes
Your half-formed 1:1 notes, exactly as you wrote them. Unpolished is fine, it's the most honest thing you'll hand over.
The competing opinions
What each person actually said, contradictions included. AI can hold three people disagreeing at once. You're usually the one who collapses it too early.

Same Problem, Two Different Ways In

Searching

"Best way to handle an underperforming employee."

Giving It the Situation

"Here are my last three 1:1 notes with this person, their last two performance ratings, and what happened in yesterday's meeting. Given all of this, what's actually going on, and what would you do first?"

The first one gets you generic advice that was already true before you typed it, and would be just as true for a thousand other managers. The second one gets you an answer built entirely out of your actual situation. Same question. Completely different conversation.

Copy This Before Your Next AI Conversation

A shape for handing over the whole problem instead of a search query.

Here's the situation: [paste the thread, the notes, the numbers, the actual mess, unedited]

Here's what I'm actually trying to figure out: [your real question]

Then ask it: "What am I missing, and what would you do first?"

The Whole Point

Instead of searching for the answer, give AI the situation. Then ask it to help you make sense of all of it. That's the new search skill.
