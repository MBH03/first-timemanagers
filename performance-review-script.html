Performance Review Script

Use this as a starting point, not something to read verbatim. Say it in your own words, but keep the order and the directness.

Set up the structure

"Before we get into specifics, here's how this review is structured and why."

Removes the anxiety of not knowing what's coming, so they can actually hear the content.

Strengths, grounded in specifics

"Here's what's working, and here's the specific example that shows it."

A generic compliment lands as filler. A specific one lands as proof.

Growth area, named directly

"Here's the one area I want you focused on this cycle, and here's why it matters."

Pick one. A list of five growth areas is not a plan, it's a burden.

Close with the throughline

"Here's how everything we just talked about connects to where you're headed."

Send them out with a direction, not just a scorecard.
