You're now managing someone who used to be your peer.

Four moves for the relationship that changed overnight — not a general essay on earning respect.

01
Name the shift directly, once
Don't pretend nothing changed. A short, honest conversation about the new dynamic beats months of both of you tiptoeing around it.
02
Stop being the last to know
You used to hear the real talk in the break room. That access is gone now, and trying to get it back by staying "one of the group" will undermine you. Build a different kind of closeness instead.
03
Treat every report the same in public
Former friends will get more scrutiny from the rest of the team than you think. Keep your fairness visible, especially with the people you're closest to.
04
Expect one relationship to get harder before it gets better
Usually your closest former peer. That's normal. Don't fix it by softening decisions that affect them differently than everyone else.
