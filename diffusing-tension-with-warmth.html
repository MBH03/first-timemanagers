The principle

Warmth isn't the opposite of directness, it's what makes directness land instead of bouncing off.

When a conversation gets heated, the instinct is either to escalate to match the energy or to go cold and formal to protect yourself. Both read as adversarial, and both make the other person defend instead of listen.

Staying warm, genuinely curious, not performatively nice, keeps the other person's guard down long enough to actually hear you. It's the difference between a conversation and a standoff, and it doesn't require softening the message itself.

Try this Monday

Next time a 1:1 gets tense, name it out loud: "I can feel this getting tense, that's not what I want, I just want to figure this out with you." Then keep going.
