Stage 02 · Day 90

The First 90 Days

Setting expectations, running your first 1:1s, and earning credibility without overcorrecting.

01
Set expectations explicitly — don't assume they're shared
Write down what "good" looks like for each person's role, and check it against what they think it is. The gap between those two lists is where most early friction comes from.
02
Build a real 1:1 rhythm
Same time, same structure, every week. The rhythm itself builds trust before any single conversation does — cancelling early trains people to expect it'll keep happening.
03
Make one visible improvement, not ten invisible ones
Pick a single small thing your team has been annoyed by and fix it fast. It buys more credibility than a strategy document nobody reads.
04
Ask for feedback on yourself before day 90 ends
You're setting the norm that feedback runs in both directions from the start. Wait too long and asking starts to feel like an event instead of a habit.
