About

Nobody hands you a manual when you become someone's boss.

First-Time Managers exists for the exact moment between getting the title and knowing what to do with it.

Most leadership advice is written for people who've already been managing for years — it assumes context you don't have yet, and it's long on theory when what you actually need is the next specific move. We built this instead: a toolkit, not a blog. A playbook you can walk through stage by stage, scripts you can copy into an actual conversation, and short lessons you can read in the time it takes to make coffee.

None of it is theoretical. Every script here is something a manager actually said, in an actual hard conversation, and it worked well enough to write down. Every playbook stage is built around the specific things that trip people up in their first year, not a general theory of leadership.

We borrow the Ted Lasso lessons on purpose. A lot of what makes someone good at leading people isn't complicated — it just isn't obvious until you see it named clearly. A fictional football coach turns out to be a surprisingly good way to name it.

If your situation isn't covered here yet, tell us. The Situation Navigator grows from real questions, and the answer we give you becomes the answer for the next person who asks.
