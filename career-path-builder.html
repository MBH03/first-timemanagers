Career Path Builder

Turn your career goal into a practical plan you can work on every month. Five short steps: your direction, your development gaps, a monthly roadmap, how to build visibility, and a plan you can print and bring to your manager.

This is your plan, not a performance review. Everything saves automatically to this browser as you type, and nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export on your finished plan before you clear your browser data or switch devices.

1. My Direction

What you're working toward, and why. You don't need a perfect answer, just a starting point.
Not fully sure yet? That's fine. Answer these to narrow it down before you build out a plan.

My Career Goal

2. My Development Gaps

For each item, mark where you stand today. Then mark up to three as your top priorities to focus on first. A short list works better than a long one.

Top development priorities

Choose up to three. These feed directly into your roadmap.

3. My Career Roadmap

One primary focus per month. This is not a promise of a promotion by a certain date, it's what you can control: learning, practice, responsibility, relationships, and results.

4. Practice and Build Visibility

For each priority: a realistic way to practice it in your current job, who could help you learn it, and how you'll make the result visible without turning it into self promotion.
Protect your current performance. Keep doing your current job well, that is what builds trust and gives you room to take on more. Start with small pieces of the next role, learn from the experience, and expand your responsibilities as you demonstrate that you can handle them.

Accomplishments log

Add entries any time, not only when you finish a monthly goal. These carry into your final plan.

Review Cadence
A short personal review every one to two weeks, and a manager conversation roughly every 30 to 60 days. At each review, ask yourself:
  • What did I accomplish?
  • What did I learn?
  • Where did I apply the skill?
  • What evidence do I have of progress?
  • What is getting in the way?
  • What should I focus on next?
