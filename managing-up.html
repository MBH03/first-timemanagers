Career Advice

Managing up, without losing yourself.

Four moves for working well with your own manager — not a general essay on office politics.

01
Learn what your manager actually gets measured on
Your priorities land better when you can connect them to what they're accountable for. Ask directly if you don't already know.
02
Bring solutions, not just problems
Show up with a recommendation, even a rough one. It's easier for a manager to react to a proposal than to generate one from scratch on your behalf.
03
Ask for feedback before it becomes a review surprise
Don't wait for the formal cycle to find out what's not landing. Ask directly and often — a surprise in a review is usually a sign you weren't asking enough.
04
Say no to the wrong things, not just yes to everything
Managing up includes protecting your own time and your team's. A well-reasoned no builds more trust over time than silent overcommitment does.
