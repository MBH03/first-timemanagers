Career Advice

Building a track record for promotion.

Four moves for making the case before you have to ask, not a general essay on being patient.

01
Document impact as it happens, not at review time
Keep a running list of wins with numbers attached. Memory is a bad archive: you will forget the specifics that made the case strong.
02
Do the level above before you ask for the title
Promotions usually confirm work that's already happening. They rarely create it. Start operating at the next level before the conversation, not after.
03
Make the ask explicit
"I want to be promoted, and here's my case" is a completely different conversation than hoping someone eventually notices. Say it directly, with a timeline attached.
04
Find a sponsor, not just a mentor
A mentor gives you advice. A sponsor advocates for you in rooms you're not in. You need both, and they're rarely the same person.
