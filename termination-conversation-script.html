Termination Conversation Script

Use this as a starting point, not something to read verbatim. Say it in your own words, but keep the order and the directness.

Opening

"I have to tell you something difficult. Today is your last day with us."

Say this in the first thirty seconds. Don't bury it under preamble.

The reason, briefly

"This decision is final, and it's based on [brief, true reason]."

Keep it short. If there's a documented history, a performance improvement plan, prior feedback, one sentence referencing it is enough: "Despite the support and timeline we agreed on, the improvement we needed didn't happen." This isn't the place to relitigate it in full.

Practical next steps

"Here's what happens next: [final pay, benefits, equipment, HR contact]."

Hand off logistics to HR or a written follow-up so you're not fielding detail questions live.

Give them space to respond

"Before we wrap up, is there anything you'd like to ask or share? I'm here to listen, the decision is final, but I want to give you space if there's something you want to say."

Let them respond fully. Listening isn't the same as reopening the decision.

Close with something true

"[Something specific and honest, if there is one.]"

Don't manufacture warmth you don't mean. A generic platitude is worse than nothing.

Manager reminders

Stay calm and confident, don't let emotion or discomfort pull you off the message. Be brief, this isn't the moment to debate or revisit the performance history. Use neutral, fact-based language, not "you failed" or "you didn't care." Let HR carry final pay, benefits, and equipment logistics. And don't discuss this with the rest of the team, that's not yours to share.

Before this meeting

Confirm every PIP, coaching note, and prior feedback conversation is documented and has been reviewed with HR. Know exactly what HR will handle afterward, final pay, benefits, equipment return, the separation agreement, and access removal, so you're not guessing live if you're asked.
