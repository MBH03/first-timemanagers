Career Advice

Returning to work after leave.

Four moves for the first weeks back, not a general essay on work-life balance.

01
Get a real handoff before you left, and a real re-onboarding when you're back
Ask directly for a written summary of what changed while you were out, instead of piecing it together in meetings over your first few weeks.
02
Don't apologize for the gap
Your value didn't reset. Framing your return as catching up rather than starting over changes how people treat you, including yourself.
03
Renegotiate your workload explicitly, don't let it happen by default
Whatever load you're handed in week one tends to stick. Have the conversation about capacity before it silently becomes the new normal.
04
Watch for the meetings you've quietly stopped being invited to
Sometimes it's an oversight, sometimes it's a real signal. Either way, it's worth asking about directly rather than wondering.
