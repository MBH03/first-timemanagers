Difficult Feedback Script

Use this as a starting point, not something to read verbatim. Say it in your own words, but keep the order and the directness.

Name it plainly

"I need to tell you something directly, and I'd rather say it than have you find out another way."

Signals this is real feedback, not small talk with a sting at the end.

The specific behavior and impact

"In [situation], what happened was [specific behavior]. Here's the impact it had."

Situation, Behavior, Impact, in that order. Name where it happened, what you actually saw them do, and what it affected. Not character. Describe what you saw, not who you think they are.

Make room to respond

"I want to hear your side before we figure out next steps."

Direct doesn't mean one-way. Let them fill in what you don't know.

Close with the ask

"Here's specifically what I need to see different, starting now."

End on the change you need, not on how the conversation felt.
