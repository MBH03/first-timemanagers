New Hire Welcome Script

Use this as a starting point, not something to read verbatim. Say it in your own words, but keep the order and the directness.

Opening

"I'm really glad you're here. Let me walk you through your first week before you dive in."

Say this before they open a single tool or ticket.

Set expectations

"For the first two weeks, your job is to learn, not to produce. Ask more questions than feels comfortable."

Removes the pressure to perform before they have context.

Introduce the support system

"Here's your onboarding buddy, go to them for the small questions you'd feel silly asking me."

New hires ask peers things they won't ask managers.

Close with a check-in commitment

"Let's talk every Friday this month about what's confusing. That's not a bad sign, that's the plan."

Normalizes confusion as expected, not a failure.
