Your first formal review cycle is coming up.

Four moves to get through it credibly — not a general essay on performance management systems.

01
Start collecting specifics now, not the week before
Vague memory produces vague reviews. Keep a running note per person as things happen, so you're transcribing instead of reconstructing when it's time to write.
02
Calibrate before you write anything final
Talk to peer managers about what "meets" versus "exceeds" actually looks like at your company before you commit words to a document. Your bar is probably off in one direction.
03
Draft the hard ones first
The review you're dreading writing is the one most likely to get rushed if you leave it for last. Get it out of the way while you still have full attention to give it.
04
Rehearse the delivery, not just the document
A well-written review delivered badly still lands badly. Say the key lines out loud before the meeting so they don't come out sideways in the room.
