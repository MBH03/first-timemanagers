Team Goals Template

Goals that only live in your head don't align a team. Write down what you're actually aiming for, how you'll know if you got there, and how anyone on the team can check the number themselves.

Two common structures for goals are SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Timely) and OKRs (Objective, Key Results). Neither is required, use whichever your team and your own leadership can actually work with without translating it every time.
