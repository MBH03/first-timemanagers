Week 1 · Move 01

Listen before you diagnose.

Spend the week watching, not deciding. Resist the urge to fix anything in week one, you don't have enough context yet to know if it's actually broken.

Arriving with a fix-it mindset usually assumes the people already there haven't already thought of your first ideas. Watching before acting isn't passivity, it's how you avoid spending your credibility solving the wrong problem in public.

Understand the "why" before you touch anything
A process that looks broken from the outside usually exists for a reason, even if that reason no longer holds. Find out what it was before you propose replacing it.
Notice who people actually go to
Informal influence rarely matches the org chart. Watch who gets pulled into decisions that aren't officially theirs.
Track what comes up more than once
One person's complaint is an opinion. The same complaint from three people in separate conversations is a pattern worth acting on.
Where to Look

Sit in on a recurring team meeting without saying much. Read through the last month of project docs, tickets, or threads. Watch how a single request actually moves from ask to done. Notice which topics people go quiet on, that's often where the real information is.
