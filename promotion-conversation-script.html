Promotion Conversation Script

Use this as a starting point, not something to read verbatim. Say it in your own words, but keep the order and the directness.

Open with the news, not the buildup

"I wanted to tell you directly, you're being promoted to [title], effective [date]."

Lead with the news itself. A long windup makes good news feel like it's being delayed.

Say why, specifically

"Here's exactly what you did that earned this: [specific accomplishments]."

Specific reasons make it feel earned. Vague praise makes it feel arbitrary.

Name what changes

"Here's what's different starting now: [scope, reports, expectations]."

Don't leave them to guess what the new role actually involves.

Close by making it theirs

"This is yours because of the work you already did, not a trial."

Removes the unspoken "prove it" that can undercut an otherwise good moment.
