Every target area needs an outcome specific enough that both of you would agree, without debate, whether it was met.

If the plan doesn't work out

Not every PIP ends in improvement, and that's what the plan is for, giving a fair, documented shot before a harder decision. If you're at the end of this one and the outcome wasn't met, You Have to Let Someone Go covers the four moves for that conversation, and the Termination Conversation, Manager Template has the script.
