Situation Navigator

Your team is remote or hybrid, and it shows.

Four moves for the problems distance actually causes, not a general essay on remote culture.

01
Replace hallway visibility with deliberate visibility
You won't see problems by accident anymore. Build check-ins that surface what you used to notice by walking past someone's desk.
02
Default to async, escalate to live
Not every update needs a meeting. Save live time for the conversations that actually need back-and-forth: feedback, conflict, ambiguity.
03
Watch for the person who's gone quiet
In an office, disengagement is visible. Remote, it's just an empty status dot. A dip in messages or a pattern of camera-off is a signal, not a coincidence.
04
Overcommunicate context, not just tasks
Remote teams miss the informal "why" that used to travel through the room. Say the reasoning out loud every time, even when it feels repetitive.
