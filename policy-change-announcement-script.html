Policy Change Announcement Script

Use this as a starting point, not something to read verbatim. Say it in your own words, but keep the order and the directness.

Opening

"I want to tell you about a change before you hear it secondhand."

Get ahead of the rumor mill.

The change and the why

"Starting [date], [specific change]. Here's the reasoning behind it: [real reason]."

People can accept an unpopular decision more easily than an unexplained one.

Acknowledge the impact

"I know this affects [specific group or way of working], and I'm not going to pretend it doesn't."

Don't spin an inconvenient change as good news.

Close with what's still open

"Here's what's fixed, and here's what I can still influence on your behalf."

Gives people a legitimate place to direct their concerns instead of just absorbing them.
