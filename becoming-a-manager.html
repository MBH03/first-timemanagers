You're not a manager yet. Here's how you get there.

The rest of this site is for after you get the job. This part is for before, the case you build, the habits you start, and the conversations that turn "I want to be a manager" into "here's why I'm ready."

The Whole Roadmap, at a Glance

The month-by-month version, from Article 9. Read the tiles below for the deep dive on each piece.

  1. Month 1: Tell your manager you want to become a manager, and ask what they'd need to see from you.
  2. Month 2: Become the person people go to for help, and volunteer for a leadership opportunity.
  3. Month 3: Lead a project involving other people, and start building relationships outside your team.
  4. Month 4: Take on cross-functional work, and practice leading without formal authority.
  5. Month 5: Deliver a measurable win, and document your results and leadership examples.
  6. Month 6: Ask for honest feedback, and have a formal halfway career conversation.
  7. Month 7: Increase your visibility through useful communication, and build relationships with other leaders.
  8. Month 8: Start demonstrating next-level behaviors.
  9. Month 9: Build your promotion case, and learn how your company's promotion and headcount process works.
  10. Month 10: Confirm the timeline for the next management opening.
  11. Month 11: Ask directly whether your manager believes you are ready.
  12. Month 12: Pursue the internal opportunity, or start looking externally.
