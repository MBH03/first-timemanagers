You're onboarding someone new to the team.

Four moves for their first month, not a general essay on culture fit.

01
Give them a first week with an actual shape
A vague "let me know if you have questions" isn't a plan. Hand them a real first-week schedule: who to meet, what to read, what to try.
02
Assign a buddy who isn't you
New hires ask more questions to a peer than to their manager. Someone who can answer the small, "dumb" questions removes friction you won't even see.
03
Give them one small real win in week one
Not a toy project. Something that ships and matters, even if it's tiny. It builds confidence faster than any amount of documentation.
04
Ask them what's confusing, every week, for the first month
New eyes catch things nobody else notices anymore. Their confusion is often a legitimate process problem, not a knowledge gap.
