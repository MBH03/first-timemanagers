Stage 01 · Week 1

Getting Oriented

What to actually do in your first five days, and what to deliberately not touch yet.

01
Listen before you diagnose
Spend the week in 1:1s and observation, not decisions. Resist the urge to fix anything in week one — you don't have enough context yet to know if it's actually broken.
02
Meet every person on your team 1:1, not as a group
Ask what's working, what's not, and what they wish someone had told them when they started. You'll hear things in a 1:1 that never surface in a team meeting.
03
Learn the actual workflow, not the org-chart version
Sit in on a real piece of work getting done and watch where it gets stuck. The org chart tells you who's involved. It doesn't tell you where the friction actually is.
04
Say what you don't know yet, out loud
Naming that you're still learning builds more trust than pretending you already have it figured out. Your team already knows you're new — pretending otherwise just costs you credibility.
